What is ECNW?

Everclear Northwest is a 1-night, 21-and-over room party for fans of My Little Pony, mixed drinks, and mildly regrettable decisions — held suspiciously close to a certain beloved all-ages convention in beautiful Bellevue, Washington.

Everclear Northwest is a fan-made parody event and private room party. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by Everfree Northwest, Pegasi Northwest, or Hasbro in any way, shape, or hoof.

Please drink responsibly. Pace yourself, hydrate, and remember that "190 proof of friendship" is a joke, not a dosage instruction.

If you're looking for the actual convention, that's everfreenw.com — go, it's great.
