Programming Notes

All drinking games use low-ABV drinks or water only — never Everclear mixes. Nopony is chugging 190 proof over a chessboard.

Every event has a sober "Designated Sylveon" host keeping an eye on the herd and the clock.

Quiet voices after midnight, please. The room next door contains a sleeping family of actual con-goers, and we respect the herd.

All species welcome: ponies, furries, and Pokémon alike — fursuit heads off during drinks, hydration applies to every fandom.
