The Mane Menu 🍹

A myriad of mixtures featuring Everclear, the Element of Chaos itself.

📜 The Royal Decree (Read Before You Trot)

Everclear is 190-proof (95% ABV) grain alcohol. It is never served neat or as shots at this party. Every single recipe below dilutes it hard — that's not a suggestion, it's the whole menu design.

One serving here = ½ oz (15 ml) Everclear ≈ one standard drink. Respect the pour lines. A jigger is not a suggestion either.

Alternate every drink with a full glass of water (see the Hydration Station). Eat real food. Know your limits — a pony who says "no thanks" is buying themselves tomorrow.

21+ only, WA legal drinking age. Never drive; the party is IN the hotel for a reason.

Signature Sippers

Pinkie Punch

gentle trot

½ oz Everclear · 3 oz fruit punch · splash grenadine · top with lemon-lime soda · maraschino cherry garnish

Shake the Everclear, punch, and grenadine with ice, strain into a glass over fresh ice, top with soda, and drop in the cherry.

Party cannon not included, but the confetti feeling absolutely is.

Twilight Sparkler

canter

½ oz Everclear · 3 oz white grape juice · splash sparkling wine or club soda · lemon twist

Build over ice, top with the sparkling pour, and give it exactly one stir — two if you're feeling studious.

Studies show this drink increases your odds of reorganizing a bookshelf by color.

Rainbow Crash

full gallop (flagship)

¾ oz Everclear · 1 oz orange juice · 1 oz pineapple juice · 1 oz cranberry juice · splash grenadine

Fill a tall glass with ice and layer the juices slowly, heaviest to lightest, so the colors stack. Everclear goes in last, gently, over the back of a spoon.

Breaks the sound barrier and possibly your Tuesday. Our only ¾ oz pour — treat it with respect.

Apple Jack & Ginger

canter

½ oz Everclear · 4 oz apple cider · 2 oz ginger beer · cinnamon stick · squeeze of lemon

Build over ice in order listed, stir once, garnish with the cinnamon stick.

Honest, hardworking, and sneaks up on you exactly like she warned you it would.

Fluttershy's Quiet Sipper

gentle trot (low-octane)

¼ oz Everclear · 4 oz chamomile-honey lemonade · splash soda water · mint sprig

Stir gently — no shaking, please, it startles easily. Serve over ice with the mint tucked in quietly.

So soft-spoken you'll barely notice it's there. That's the entire point.

Rarity Spritz

gentle trot

½ oz Everclear · 3 oz sparkling white grape juice · splash rosé lemonade · lime wheel

Pour over ice in a glass that matches your outfit. Top with the sparkling grape juice last, for the bubbles.

Dahling, it's simply divine, and the glass positively matches your eyes.

Sonic Rainboom

canter

½ oz Everclear · 2 oz blue electrolyte sports drink · 3 oz lemon-lime soda · splash blue raspberry syrup

Build over ice, top with soda last for the fizz, and garnish with a rainbow candy if you have one on hand.

Breaks Mach 1. Recommended consumption speed: subsonic.

Derpy's Muffin Mule

canter

½ oz Everclear · 4 oz ginger beer · ½ oz lime juice · lime wheel garnish

Build over ice in a mug (copper if you've got one), give it one stir, garnish with the lime.

Delivers deliciousness right to your table. Some spillage expected, all of it forgiven.

Celestia's Sun Tea

gentle trot

½ oz Everclear · 4 oz sweet iced tea · 1 oz lemonade · honey to taste · lemon wheel

Stir the honey into the warm tea first so it dissolves, then build the rest over ice.

Radiant, warm, and rules the day for a thousand years — or at least tonight.

Luna's Moonlit Lemonade

canter

½ oz Everclear · 4 oz blueberry lemonade · splash soda water · blackberry garnish

Build over ice, top with soda, drop in the blackberries. Best enjoyed under dim lighting for maximum mystique.

Comes out at night, rules the moon, and occasionally raises the dead — the party, that is.

Changeling

canter

½ oz Everclear · 4 oz mixer of your choice (whatever juice, soda, or iced tea is on the table) · garnish to match

Pick any mixer on the bar, shake with ice, strain. It becomes whatever the room needs it to be.

Tastes like love. Or juice. Honestly, mostly juice.

Cider Season

canter

½ oz Everclear · 5 oz warm apple cider · cinnamon stick · star anise · orange peel

Warm the cider gently (do not boil — you'll cook off the fun), stir in the Everclear off heat, garnish and serve in a mug.

It's always cider season somewhere. Here, it's always right now.

Foal-Friendly (Designated Sylveon)

zero proof

No Everclear · 4 oz cranberry juice · 2 oz ginger ale · splash lime juice · grenadine drizzle · cherry garnish

Build over ice, top with ginger ale, drizzle grenadine down the side so it sinks, and drop in the cherry.

All the sparkle, none of the spellcheck-required texts tomorrow. For designated drivers and anypony who'd rather not, no questions asked.

The Punch Bowls

Grand Guzzling Gala Punch

batch · serves 16

1 cup (8 oz) Everclear · 2 quarts fruit punch · 1 quart ginger ale · 2 cups pineapple juice · 1 cup grenadine · sliced oranges, lemons, and frozen berries

Combine everything except the soda in a large punch bowl and chill. Add the ginger ale and a fruit-and-ice ring right before the doors open. Ladle, don't dunk.

Math: 1 cup Everclear across 16 servings = ½ oz per serving.

Everfree Forest Jungle Juice, But Responsible

batch · serves 24

¾ cup (6 oz) Everclear · 1 gallon mixed tropical juice (pineapple, orange, mango) · 2 liters lemon-lime soda · splash grenadine · frozen fruit chunks for the ice ring

Mix the juices and Everclear in a large cooler or bowl, chill thoroughly, then float the soda and fruit-studded ice ring on top just before serving.

Math: 6 oz Everclear across 24 servings = ¼ oz per serving. It looks dangerous. It is not.

Cloudsdale Cooler Bowl

batch · serves 32

1 cup (8 oz) Everclear · 1 gallon lemonade · 1 liter sparkling water · 2 cups white grape juice · sliced lemons and mint

Stir everything but the sparkling water together and chill for at least an hour. Add the sparkling water right at service so it stays fizzy through the whole gala.

Math: 8 oz Everclear across 32 servings = ¼ oz per serving — a light, all-afternoon bowl for a big herd.

House Rules of the Menu

  • Measure every single pour with an actual jigger. Eyeballing it is how "½ oz" becomes "a problem."
  • No drink-stacking — finish (or water-break) before you start the next one.
  • Every punch bowl gets a ladle and a sign listing every ingredient, for allergies and informed consent. No sign, no serving.
  • A cut-off is a kindness, not an insult. If someone calls it for you, say thanks and switch to water.
